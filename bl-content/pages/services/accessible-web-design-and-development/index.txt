<p>A website should work well for everyone who needs to use it. I design and build custom websites that meet WCAG 2.2 AA standards from the start, rather than trying to fix accessibility issues later.</p>
<p>Every project prioritises:</p>
<ul>
<li>Semantic HTML and clear structure</li>
<li>Full keyboard accessibility and visible focus states</li>
<li>Sufficient colour contrast and readable typography</li>
<li>Responsive layouts that work across devices and zoom levels</li>
<li>Compatible behaviour with screen readers and other assistive technologies</li>
</ul>
<p>Performance and usability are considered alongside accessibility, so the finished site is fast, reliable, and straightforward to maintain.</p>
<p>Whether you need a new website or a redesign of an existing one, the process is collaborative. We define requirements together, review designs and prototypes for accessibility early, and deliver a site that is both compliant and practical for your organisation and its users.</p>
