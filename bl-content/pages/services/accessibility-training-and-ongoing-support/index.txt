<p>Creating accessible digital content is an ongoing process. From publishing website content to preparing documents and implementing website updates, everyone involved has a role to play in making digital experiences accessible.</p>
<p>I provide practical, tailored training and ongoing support to help your team understand accessibility requirements, develop the necessary skills and apply accessibility best practices in their everyday work.</p>
<h2>What I can help with</h2>
<p>My training and support services can be tailored to your organisation's needs and the roles of your team members, including:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Accessible content creation</strong>: Helping content editors create accessible web pages, write meaningful alternative text and structure content clearly.</li>
<li><strong>Accessible documents</strong>: Training your team to create accessible Word documents, PDFs and presentations, reducing the need for corrections later.</li>
<li><strong>Web accessibility fundamentals</strong>: Explaining key accessibility principles and WCAG requirements in a clear, practical way.</li>
<li><strong>Accessible website management</strong>: Helping your team maintain accessibility when editing content, adding new pages or using content management systems such as WordPress.</li>
<li><strong>Developer guidance</strong>: Supporting developers in understanding accessibility requirements and applying good practices when building or updating websites and components.</li>
<li><strong>Ongoing advice and support</strong>: Providing guidance on accessibility questions, helping your team address issues and offering practical recommendations as your digital content evolves.</li>
<li><strong>Periodic accessibility checks</strong>: Reviewing selected pages, documents or website changes to identify potential issues and help prevent existing accessibility problems from being reintroduced.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Keeping accessibility on track</h2>
<p>Accessibility doesn't end after a website has been launched or an initial audit has been completed. New content, design changes, software updates and third-party integrations can all introduce new accessibility barriers.</p>
<p>Ongoing support helps your organisation maintain accessibility over time through regular guidance, periodic checks and assistance with emerging issues.</p>
<p>The aim is to help your team become more confident and independent in managing accessibility, reduce recurring issues and build more inclusive digital experiences for everyone.</p>
